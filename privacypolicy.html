Privacy Policy Last Updated: 2026-06-02 Corient Business Solutions Limited ("we", "us", "our") operates the MTD Pro mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the App. MTD Pro is a Making Tax Digital (MTD) compliance tool that helps UK self-employed individuals and landlords meet their legal obligations under HMRC's MTD for Income Tax Self Assessment (MTD ITSA) programme. By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App. 1. Who We Are Data Controller: Corient Business Solutions Limited The Techno Center, Puma Way Coventry, CV1 2TT United Kingdom Company Registration: England and Wales Email: info@corientbs.co.uk Note on Data Roles: Where the App is used by an accounting practice to manage client data, the practice acts as the Data Controller and we act as the Data Processor, processing data only on the practice's instructions. 2. Information We Collect We collect the following categories of personal data: Identity Data: First name, last name, username. Contact Data: Email address, telephone number. Business Data: Business name, address, Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR), National Insurance number, practice details. Financial Data: Income records, expense records, property rental income, bank transaction data you choose to import. Document Data: Receipts, invoices, and other documents you upload. HMRC Submission Data: Quarterly updates and final declarations submitted to HMRC on your behalf. Technical Data: Device type, operating system version, app version, crash reports, and usage analytics. Authentication Data: Encrypted login credentials stored in the device Keychain; we never store plain-text passwords. 3. How We Use Your Information We use your personal data exclusively to: Provide and operate the MTD Pro service. Create and manage your account. Prepare and submit quarterly updates and final declarations to HMRC via the Making Tax Digital API. Generate financial reports (profit & loss, balance sheet, trial balance). Send important service notifications (submission reminders, HMRC deadlines). Resolve technical issues and improve app performance. Comply with UK tax law, HMRC requirements, and legal obligations. Data Commercialisation Policy: We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party. 4. Legal Basis for Processing (UK GDPR) We process your personal data under the following lawful bases: Contract: Processing is necessary to provide the service you have requested and to fulfil our contractual obligations to you. Legal Obligation: Processing is required to comply with UK tax law, HMRC regulations, and the MTD ITSA mandate. Legitimate Interests: Processing is necessary for our legitimate interests in operating a secure and reliable service, improving the App, and preventing fraud — provided those interests are not overridden by your rights. Consent: Where we rely on consent (e.g., optional analytics), you may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us. 5. Sharing Your Information We share personal data only where necessary: HMRC: We transmit your tax data to HMRC via the official MTD API as directed by you when you authorise a submission. Your Accountant / Practice: If you are a client of an accounting practice using MTD Pro, the practice has access to your data as Data Controller. Infrastructure Providers: Our hosting and cloud infrastructure providers process data on our behalf under data processing agreements with appropriate safeguards. Legal Requirements: We may disclose your data to law enforcement or regulatory bodies when required by law. Third-Party Restriction: We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or social media platforms. 6. Data Retention We retain your personal data for as long as: Your account remains active, or It is required to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or UK tax law and HMRC regulations require retention (typically 5 years from the 31 January submission deadline for the relevant tax year). When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymised. You may request earlier deletion subject to legal retention requirements — see "Your Rights" below. 7. Your Rights Under UK GDPR You have the following rights regarding your personal data: Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data. Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data where there is no lawful reason to continue processing (subject to tax law retention requirements). Right to Restriction: Request that we limit processing of your data in certain circumstances. Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format. Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests. Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent. How to Exercise Your Rights To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@corientbs.co.uk or use the account deletion option in the app (More → Profile → Delete Account). 8. Data Security We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data: All data is transmitted using TLS 1.2+ encryption (HTTPS). Authentication tokens are stored in the device's secure Keychain (iOS) or Keystore (Android), accessible only when the device is unlocked. The app enforces portrait-only display and does not cache sensitive data in screenshots. Access to backend systems is controlled by role-based access and multi-factor authentication. We maintain audit logs of all HMRC submissions. Disclaimer: No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We take reasonable steps to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security. 9. Children's Privacy MTD Pro is intended for use by adults who have UK tax obligations. The App is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at info@corientbs.co.uk and we will delete it. 10. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the law or our practices. When we make material changes, we will notify you via the App or by email before the changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. 11.
